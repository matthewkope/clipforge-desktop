# ClipForge — Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 2026-06-10**

This Privacy Policy explains how the ClipForge desktop application and optional
browser extension (the "Software") handle information. The short version:
**ClipForge runs entirely on your own computer and does not send your personal
data, browsing activity, or downloaded content to us or to any third party we
control.**

---

## 1. We Do Not Operate a Server That Collects Your Data

ClipForge is a local desktop application. We do not run accounts, cloud storage,
analytics back-ends, or telemetry servers that receive your information. We have
no ability to see what URLs you analyze, what you download, or what files you
create.

## 2. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

To do its job, the Software processes the following **only on your device**:

- **URLs you paste or send** — analyzed locally and passed to the bundled
  download tools so they can fetch the media you requested.
- **Files you download or create** — saved to the folder you choose. They stay
  on your computer.
- **App settings** — such as your selected save folder, output preferences, and
  extension presets, stored locally in the application's data directory.
- **Diagnostic logs** — the app may write local debug logs (for example, a
  Pinterest debug log) to help you troubleshoot. These stay on your device and
  are designed **not** to record cookie contents or passwords.

## 3. Browser Cookies

For content that requires you to be logged in, the Software can, **at your
direction**, read cookies from a browser already installed on your computer (for
example via the operating system keychain) or from a `cookies.txt` file you
select. This is done **locally** and only to authenticate your own download
requests to the relevant platform.

- Cookies are used solely to perform the download you asked for.
- Any temporary cookie file the app creates is written with owner-only
  permissions and is **deleted when the app exits**.
- The Software does not collect your account passwords, and we never receive
  your cookies — they never leave your machine except in the network request the
  download tool makes directly to the platform you are downloading from.

## 4. Network Connections

The only network connections the Software makes are:

- Connections made by the bundled download tools **directly to the third-party
  website or platform you chose** (e.g., to fetch a video you requested).
- A **local-only** connection between the optional browser extension and the
  desktop app over `127.0.0.1` (loopback). This never leaves your computer.

The Software does not "phone home" to us.

## 5. Third-Party Platforms

When you download from a third-party platform (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, etc.),
your request goes to that platform, and that platform's own privacy practices
apply to that interaction. We are not responsible for, and have no control over,
how those platforms handle your data.

## 6. Children's Privacy

The Software is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect personal
information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in
your jurisdiction).

## 7. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. The "Last updated" date above reflects the
current version. Material changes will be reflected in an updated release.
